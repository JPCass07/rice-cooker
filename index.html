Rice Cooker. Open source, MIT. Colours and scripts are made in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Presets credit the rices they are based on: Serpantinum by ilyamiro, Caelestia by soramane, and the Hyprland ricing competition winners listed in the hall of fame.
