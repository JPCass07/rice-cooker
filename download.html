Take it home.
Rice Cooker for your desktop.

The app cooks rices straight onto KDE Plasma 6, keeps the ones you save, switches between them in one click, and opens .rice files people send you. Every change is backed up first.

Download rice-cooker.flatpak Use it in the browser instead

01

Install

Needs Flatpak with Flathub. The GNOME runtime it runs on comes from Flathub automatically.

flatpak install --user rice-cooker.flatpak
02

Unbox

On first launch it finds your distro and Plasma version, backs up your desktop, and lets you pick a first rice.

flatpak run io.github.jpcass07.RiceCooker
03

Cook and switch

Cook it applies a rice with a live log. Save rices to My rices and switch with one click; restore any backup.

Good to know

Runs on
KDE Plasma 6 on Debian 13, Kubuntu 26.04, Fedora 43, Arch and openSUSE Tumbleweed
Tested
Debian 13 with Plasma 6.3; scripts for the others are checked in containers
Not sandboxed
It runs the rice script on your computer (flatpak-spawn --host), only the script it shows you, after a backup
Passwords
Only when packages are missing, through a system dialog that hands it straight to sudo
Remove it
flatpak uninstall --user io.github.jpcass07.RiceCooker
